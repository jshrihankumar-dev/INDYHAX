# UI Direction

## Design Goal

The interface should feel calm, personal, and emotionally safe. Avoid a generic AI dashboard look.

## Visual Concept

Use a split layout:

- Left side: the user's context form
- Right side: generated message card
- Center or background: subtle bridge arc

## Mood

- Warm
- Human
- Reflective
- Hopeful without being cheesy

## Color Direction

- Soft cream or warm gray background
- Deep navy or forest green text
- Amber or muted gold accent for bridge highlights
- Avoid harsh red except for errors

## Typography Direction

Use a distinctive heading font if possible. Body text should be readable and calm.

## Motion

- Bridge fades in when result appears
- Message card rises slightly into place
- Copy confirmation uses toast
- Avoid excessive animation that makes the emotional topic feel unserious
