# Bridge Theme

## Literal Interpretation

The app uses a bridge visual to represent a message traveling from the user to the person they lost contact with.

## Emotional Interpretation

The bridge is the first message after silence.

## Social Impact Interpretation

The bridge reconnects people who might otherwise remain isolated.

## Pitch Language

- "A bridge back to someone you miss."
- "One message can cross years of silence."
- "We are not replacing relationships with AI. We are using AI to help people restart human relationships."

## Visual Metaphor

Left side: the user

Right side: the person they lost contact with

Arc bridge: the generated message
