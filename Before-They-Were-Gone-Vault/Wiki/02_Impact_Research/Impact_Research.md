# Impact Research

## Impact Thesis

Before They Were Gone addresses social disconnection by helping people take the first step toward reconnection. The impact is strongest when framed around loneliness, isolation, and the difficulty of rebuilding social ties.

## Research Notes

### Loneliness Is a Public Health Issue

CDC research describes loneliness and social isolation as indicators of social disconnection that are linked with worse mental and physical health outcomes, including depression, anxiety, dementia risk, cardiovascular disease risk, and premature mortality.

Source: https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/73/wr/mm7324a1.htm

### Many Adults Feel Lonely

Harvard Graduate School of Education's Making Caring Common project reported in 2024 that 21% of adults in the United States feel lonely.

Source: https://mcc.gse.harvard.edu/reports/loneliness-in-america-2024

### Older Adults Are Especially Vulnerable

JAMA notes that older adults face increased risk of social isolation and loneliness because of factors like loss of loved ones, decreased mobility, chronic illness, transportation barriers, and reduced access to favorite activities.

Source: https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2819153

## Why This Project Matters

The project does not claim to solve loneliness alone. Its practical value is narrower and more believable: it lowers the barrier to making one reconnection attempt.

## Judge-Friendly Impact Framing

"Loneliness is a public health issue, but reconnection often starts with one small message. Our app helps people cross that first emotional bridge."

## Ethical Notes

- The app should not pressure users to reconnect with unsafe people.
- The warning section should normalize the possibility of no response.
- The app should encourage low-pressure and respectful messages.
- Sensitive relationship data should not be stored in the MVP.
