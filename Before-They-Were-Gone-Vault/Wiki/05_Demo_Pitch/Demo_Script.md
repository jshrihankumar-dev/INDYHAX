# Demo Script

## Setup

"For the demo, imagine I moved away from my childhood best friend three years ago. We were close, but we stopped texting and now reaching out feels awkward."

## Steps

1. Click "Try an Example."
2. Show the completed form.
3. Point out closeness, reason, duration, and tone.
4. Click generate.
5. Wait for the bridge animation and result card.
6. Read the generated message.
7. Read the "why this works" line.
8. Read the warning.
9. Click copy.

## Closing Line

"The bridge is not the AI. The bridge is the message it helps you finally send."
