# Judge Pitch

## 30-Second Pitch

Everyone has someone they have lost contact with and never reached out to because they did not know what to say. **Before They Were Gone** helps people build that bridge back. You enter the relationship, how long it has been, why you drifted, and the tone you want. The app generates a short human message, explains why it works, and gives an honest warning about what to expect. It turns emotional hesitation into one respectful first step.

## One-Liner

Before They Were Gone builds the bridge back to someone you miss, one message at a time.

## Impact Framing

Loneliness and social disconnection are real public health concerns. This project does not solve them completely, but it helps with a concrete first action: reaching out.

## Technical Framing

We built a React app that uses structured prompting with Claude to generate a reconnection message, explanation, and emotional warning as parseable JSON.
