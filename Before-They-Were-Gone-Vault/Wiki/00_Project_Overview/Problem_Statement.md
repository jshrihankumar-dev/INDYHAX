# Problem Statement

## Core Problem

People often lose contact with friends, family, classmates, teammates, or mentors. Reaching back out can feel awkward, risky, or emotionally loaded.

## Why It Happens

- Too much time has passed
- The user does not know what tone to use
- The relationship ended with tension
- The user is afraid of being ignored
- The user overthinks the first message
- A generic message feels fake

## Why Current Solutions Are Weak

Generic AI chatbots can write messages, but they do not structure the user experience around reconnection. They also may produce messages that are too long, too formal, too dramatic, or obviously AI-generated.

## Product Opportunity

Build a focused tool that turns relationship context into one small, sendable message. The app should feel safe, simple, and emotionally intelligent.
