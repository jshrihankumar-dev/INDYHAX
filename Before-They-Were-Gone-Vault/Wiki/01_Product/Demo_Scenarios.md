# Demo Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Childhood Best Friend

- Person: Childhood best friend
- Relationship: Friend
- Closeness: 9/10
- Why they drifted: Moved away and stopped texting regularly
- Time apart: 3 years
- Tone: Warm

## Scenario 2: Old Teammate

- Person: Old soccer teammate
- Relationship: Former teammate
- Closeness: 7/10
- Why they drifted: Season ended and everyone got busy
- Time apart: 8 months
- Tone: Casual

## Scenario 3: Cousin After Family Tension

- Person: Cousin
- Relationship: Family
- Closeness: 6/10
- Why they drifted: Family tension made conversations awkward
- Time apart: 2 years
- Tone: Warm

## Best Demo Pick

Use Scenario 1 if the judges need an emotional, simple example. It is easy to understand and fits the bridge theme clearly.
