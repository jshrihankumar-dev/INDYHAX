# Before They Were Gone Vault

This vault contains the project wiki, build plan, impact research, demo plan, and todo list for the hackathon project **Before They Were Gone**.

## Project Summary

**Before They Were Gone** helps people reconnect with someone they lost contact with by generating a thoughtful, human message they can send. The project fits the hackathon theme **Bridge** by helping users build a bridge back to a person, relationship, or community connection.

Everyone has someone they have lost contact with and never reached out to because they did not know what to say. **Before They Were Gone** builds the bridge back, one message at a time.
